## What's the idea here?

If you don't know yet, there is so many sorting algorithm for the one sorting array problem, bubble sort, binary sort, merge sort, et cetera. How do we know which sorting algorithm do we need for our problem? or which kind of sorting algorithm is the best so we don't have to choose? Imagine you have the task to punch the nail in the wood. Which kind of instrument you want to pack a punch to the nail. Is it a hammer? a drill? a screwdriver? or a spoon? You may have one in your mind already and you already knew why you want to use it but there is ONE tools that suited the best to puch the nail and The WHY behind the reason which instrument is best is what BigO came in the world of algorithm. WHY do we think this algorithm is best. We determined via BigO.
